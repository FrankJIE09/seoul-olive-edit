# Seoul edit

Audience: a Chinese-speaking traveler shopping in Korean Olive Young stores, buying for themselves and friends. Product fit, Korean shelf names and honest sources matter more than hype.

Palette: paper #fafbf8, white #ffffff, ink #20271e, leaf #356345, lime #d7ef88, blush #f7e7e9. Lime is reserved for selected states and the shopping list. Product packaging supplies most of the color.

Type: system sans with PingFang SC and Apple SD Gothic Neo for Chinese/Korean; Georgia only for the short English wordmark. Fixed type scale 12, 14, 16, 20, 28, 40px. No negative tracking or viewport-scaled text.

Layout: a compact retail notebook, left aligned. Desktop has a narrow filter rail and three product columns; mobile has horizontal categories and two columns. A side drawer holds the shopping list. Notes and store advice are separate tab views.

    wordmark | products / gifts / notes / visit | list
    Olive Young shopping guide        research date
    purpose switch | search
    filters | product | product | product

Review: avoid a generic marketing hero or landscape photograph. The first viewport contains actual packages and price estimates, with restrained editorial typography. No decorative cards around page sections. Cards only represent individual products. Source labels distinguish official listings, readable social text and the editor's own recommendations. No fabricated rankings, review counts, stock availability or promotional prices.

Use the GitHub skill anthropics/skills, skills/frontend-design (installed locally as frontend-design). Use buildless HTML/CSS/JavaScript so the guide is portable and requires no service to retain a local shopping list.
